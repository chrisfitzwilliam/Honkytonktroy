A little about us

Welcome to the Tonk.

— y'all sit anywhere —

Honky Tonk Troy is your go-to spot for an authentic country western experience on Main Street. Great music, southern-inspired food, and the kind of exceptional service that keeps the night moving. Our live bands play classic and modern country hits all week long.

Same family as the Main Street HonkyTonk over in St. Charles — different room, different town, same idea. Pull up a stool.

"Live country, cold whiskey, smash burgers, and a back room loud enough to lose your voice in." ★ what we're shooting for, anyway
