Our Story

Where Troy Comes
to Let Loose

Honky Tonk Troy was born from one simple idea: Troy, Missouri deserved a true roadhouse — a place where the music's loud, the beer's cold, and nobody leaves without making a friend.

Tucked right in the heart of Lincoln County, we're part dance hall, part BBQ joint, and full-time good times. Whether you're a boot-scootin' regular or just passing through on Route 61, you'll feel at home the moment you walk through our saloon doors.

We partner with local pitmasters, stock Missouri craft brews alongside the classics, and book everything from up-and-coming local acts to nationally-touring country artists. This is your bar — pull up a barstool.
